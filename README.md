Features  
​Adjustable group size: choose 1 to 50 dancers with a slider. Each number of dancers has its own set of 50 ready-made formations (starting with Straight Line).  
​Automatic transitions: formations change on their own, with a selectable interval of 3, 5, or 8 seconds.  
​Playback controls: Prev, Next, and Pause buttons, plus a menu to jump straight to any formation. Keyboard shortcuts: Space to pause or play, and the left and right arrow keys to move between formations.  
​Customizable dancer look: display dancers as a Dot, Number, Emoji, Initial, or Name. For names, the user types one name per line.  
​Color palettes: Rainbow, Sunset, Ocean, or One color.  
​Choose formations: tick only the formations to play. If none are ticked, all 50 are played.  
​Full screen mode: shows the stage on a TV or projector for group rehearsals.  
​Built-in guide: a "How to use" panel explains every control.  
​How It Works / How to Use  
​1. Set your dancers. Use the slider under the stage to pick 1 to 50 dancers.  
​2. Pick a look. Choose Dot, Number, Emoji, Initial, or Name, then a color palette.  
​3. Watch it play. The formation changes automatically. Choose 3s, 5s, or 8s as the time between moves.  
​4. Take control. Use Prev, Next, or Pause, or jump to a formation from the menu.  
​5. Choose your formations. Press "Choose formations", tick the ones you want, then press Done.  
​6. Rehearse big. Press Full screen to display the stage on a TV or projector.
