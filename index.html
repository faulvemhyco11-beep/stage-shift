How to use Stage Shift

Stage Shift is a formation planner for dance groups. It shows your dancers on a floor and moves them through 50 formations.

  1. Set your dancers. Use the slider under the stage to pick 1 to 50 dancers.
  2. Pick a look. Choose Dot, Number, Emoji, Initial or Name, then a color palette. For names, type one per line.
  3. Watch it play. The formation changes by itself. Choose 3s, 5s or 8s for the time between moves.
  4. Take control. Use Prev, Next or Pause, or jump to a formation from the menu. On a keyboard, use Space and the left and right arrow keys.
  5. Choose your formations. Press "Choose formations", tick only the ones you want, then press Done. Only those will play.
  6. Rehearse big. Press Full screen to show the stage on a TV or projector.
